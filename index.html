Before you begin

No account, no cloud

Reports and your identity are stored in this browser only. Nothing is sent to a server. Clearing your browser data removes everything.

How inviting works

The first time you connect two devices, one shows a QR code and the other scans it. After that, the devices recognise each other and connect automatically whenever both are open — no scanning needed again.

Direct and encrypted

Data moves directly between devices, encrypted end-to-end by WebRTC. A small signaling service helps the two devices find each other, but your reports never pass through it.

A prototype from TU Delft

This is a demonstration. Treat it as a working draft rather than a production tool.
